## Post-process contract

- Accept only a source video on the exact `1024 × 1024` canvas at no less than `24` fps.
- Extract exactly `18` forward images at `6` fps before loop processing; trim any tolerated excess duration and reject a short clip.
- Resize every extracted image to `256 × 256`. Keep a shared fixed canvas and lower central root/base anchor across all extracted images.
- Use the `ping_pong` loop treatment defined above.
- Pack the extracted images into an atlas with no more than eight columns.
- Play the resulting atlas at `6` fps in Godot.
