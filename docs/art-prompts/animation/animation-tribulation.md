## Post-process contract

- Accept only a source video on the exact `1024 × 1536` canvas at no less than `24` fps.
- Extract exactly `20` forward images at `10` fps before loop processing; trim any tolerated excess duration and reject a short clip.
- Resize every extracted image to `256 × 384`. Keep the full overlay canvas and scene attachment coordinates fixed across all extracted images.
- Use the `seam_blend` loop treatment defined above.
- Pack the extracted images into an atlas with no more than eight columns.
- Play the resulting atlas at `10` fps in Godot.
